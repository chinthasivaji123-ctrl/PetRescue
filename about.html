About PetRescue

Helping animals find safety, care and loving homes.

What is PetRescue?

PetRescue is a web-based platform designed to help people report and find lost, injured and abandoned pets.

The platform provides a simple way for people to share information about animals that need help and connect with people who can provide rescue, care, shelter or adoption support.

🐾

Our Goal

To make pet rescue information easier to discover and encourage people to help animals in need.

Our Main Objectives

📢

Easy Reporting

Make it easy for people to report animals that need help.

🔎

Easy Discovery

Help people find information about pets that need rescue or adoption.

❤️

Animal Welfare

Encourage responsible care, rescue and adoption of animals.
